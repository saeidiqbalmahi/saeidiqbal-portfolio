About me

Operator first,
marketer by craft.

I’m most useful where business, technology and customers meet — setting up the store, the system behind it, and the marketing that brings people to it.

A short story

I started in content — editing video at Mangrove Institute of Science and Technology in 2022–23 — which taught me how attention works. I moved into business operations as Executive Officer at FSN IT Solution, then into marketing leadership at Believer, and now I run e-commerce and digital operations as General Manager at Marks Avenue.

Alongside my day job I build things myself: an agriculture project with Baraka Foundation (vegetable farming and sales through autumn 2026) and My Choice Tissue, a consumer tissue business with dealer, trade and retail pricing. Building my own ventures keeps my advice practical — I care about stock, margins, delivery and repeat customers, not just clicks.

My current direction is deeper into technology and cybersecurity alongside e-commerce — how stores stay fast, secure and trustworthy as they scale.
